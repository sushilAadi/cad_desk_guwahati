## 7. Reviews (within Google's policy)

Rules: ask every student, not only happy ones. Never offer discounts, gifts or anything else in exchange for a review. Never write reviews for yourselves, and don't review-gate (check if someone is happy before sending the link). Reply to every review within a few days.

Get your review link from Business Profile, under **Ask for reviews**.

**WhatsApp/SMS after the course:**
> Hi {name}, thank you for completing {course} at CAD Desk Guwahati. If you have a minute, we'd value an honest review of your experience: {review_link}. It helps other students choose. Thank you!

**Reply to a positive review:**
> Thank you, {name}! We're glad {course} was useful. All the best with your projects. – CAD Desk Guwahati

**Reply to a negative review:**
> Thank you for the feedback, {name}. We're sorry this didn't meet your expectations. Please call us on +91 86382 70013 so we can understand what happened and put it right. – CAD Desk Guwahati
