## Part 1: Google Search Console (Day 1, about 20 minutes)

This tells Google the website exists and asks it to read all the pages.

### 1A. Add the website
- [ ] On a computer, open **https://search.google.com/search-console**
- [ ] Sign in with the business Gmail account. Use the same account for everything in this guide.
- [ ] Click **Add property** (top left, the dropdown arrow).
- [ ] Two boxes appear. In the **right** box, **URL prefix**, type `https://caddesk.neeed.in` and click **Continue**.

### 1B. Prove you own the website
Google shows a list of ways to verify.
- [ ] Open **HTML tag** and copy the line it shows. It looks like `<meta name="google-site-verification" content="abc123..." />`
- [ ] **Send that line to the website developer (Sushil).** He adds it to the site and pushes the update.
- [ ] Once he says it's live, go back and click **Verify**.
- [ ] You should see **"Ownership verified"**. If it fails, wait 10 minutes and click Verify again.

### 1C. Submit the sitemap
The sitemap is the list of every page on the site: 72 pages right now.
- [ ] In the left menu, click **Sitemaps**.
- [ ] Under "Add a new sitemap", type `sitemap.xml`, so the full address reads `https://caddesk.neeed.in/sitemap.xml`
- [ ] Click **Submit**.
- [ ] The status should say **Success** (it can take a day). If it says "Couldn't fetch", wait a day and try again.

### 1D. Ask Google to read the most important pages first
- [ ] Click the search bar at the very top ("Inspect any URL").
- [ ] Paste each address below, press Enter, wait, then click **Request indexing**. Do them one at a time, since each takes about a minute.
  - [ ] `https://caddesk.neeed.in`
  - [ ] `https://caddesk.neeed.in/courses`
  - [ ] `https://caddesk.neeed.in/enquiry`
  - [ ] `https://caddesk.neeed.in/courses/autocad` *(if it says the page doesn't exist, open the website, click the AutoCAD course, and copy the address from the browser)*
  - [ ] The Revit course page
  - [ ] The SOLIDWORKS course page
  - [ ] The 3ds Max course page: `https://caddesk.neeed.in/courses/3ds-max`

Google limits how many pages you can request in a day. If it says "Quota exceeded", do the rest tomorrow.

### 1E. Check back (after 1 week)
- [ ] Open Search Console, then **Pages** in the left menu.
- [ ] The green **Indexed** number should be going up.
- [ ] Check the grey **Not indexed** list. If any course pages are in it, send a screenshot to Sushil.

## Part 3: Reviews (start this week, then keep going forever)

More recent, genuine reviews are the biggest thing that moves you up on Google Maps.

### 3A. Get your review link
- [ ] In business.google.com, click **Ask for reviews** (or "Get more reviews").
- [ ] Copy the short link and save it in your phone notes. Call it `{review_link}` below.

### 3B. Ask every student
Send this on WhatsApp **to every student** on the last day of their course, not just the happy ones:

> Hi {name}, thank you for completing {course} at CAD Desk Guwahati. If you have a minute, we'd value an honest review of your experience: {review_link}. It helps other students choose. Thank you!

- [ ] Also ask students who finished in the last 6 months. Send the same message.
- [ ] Put a printed QR code of the review link at reception. Make one with any free QR code maker.

**Do not:**
- offer discounts, gifts or certificates in exchange for reviews
- ask friends, family or staff to post reviews
- ask "were you happy?" first and only send the link to the ones who say yes
- post reviews yourself

Google removes these reviews and can penalise the listing.

### 3C. Reply to every review, within 2–3 days
**Good review:**
> Thank you, {name}! We're glad {course} was useful. All the best with your projects. – CAD Desk Guwahati

**Bad review** (stay calm and never argue):
> Thank you for the feedback, {name}. We're sorry this didn't meet your expectations. Please call us on +91 86382 70013 so we can understand what happened and put it right. – CAD Desk Guwahati

- [ ] If a review is fake or abusive, click the three dots next to it and choose **Report review**. Never reply angrily.
